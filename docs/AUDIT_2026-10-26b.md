### Statistics and method

| # | Finding | Sev. |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **SMF passes rest on an untested condition.** In §8, §10, §11 the shift-null p equals its floor p_min (0.0087–0.0114) in every PASS, so the null cannot tell strong from weak alignment. The deciding condition, MSE_model < MSE_zero, is a point comparison with no significance test, on overlapping multi-step forecasts (autocorrelated errors). A Diebold–Mariano test with HAC variance is the standard check and was not run. "SMF detects forecast fidelity at 3–6 months" is therefore stated more strongly than tested. | A |
| S2 | SMF tests §7–§12 were designed one after another, each after seeing the last result (CO2 lead 12 → leads → lead 18 → electricity → SPF → IP), about 15 verdicts in 90 minutes. Each was pre-registered, but the summary in README/STATUS reads across them with no correction for the adaptive sequence. | B |
| S3 | **RDL held-out tests were not blind.** Protocols, inputs and runs for the backtest and v0.3 were written within ~10–20 minutes each on 20 Oct, on historical cases (leaded petrol, sulphur 1985, chrysotile 2004) whose outcomes the author and the AI knew. Time order was kept; blindness to outcomes was not. Both failed, so no false pass, but the failures and any future pass should be labelled "pre-registered on known history". The forecast ledger (F001–F005) is the only blind RDL test. | B |
| S4 | GWI pass criterion 3 treats a non-significant placebo (p = 0.061 vs 0.05) as evidence of no placebo effect; with Fisher's test optimistic under clustering (first audit #5) the placebo margin means little either way. Verdict unaffected; the design should change for any future GWI test. | B |
| S5 | One-off reruns (`one_off.yml`) refetch current data vintages; SMF and synergy recorded results are not in the weekly frozen-output check, so nothing shows they still reproduce from the committed data. | B |
