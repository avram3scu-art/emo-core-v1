TITLE: Coronavirus - Delivery of medical equipment from RescEU Reserve in ItalyOn 25 April 2020, FFP2 protective masks from the RescEU Reserve were delivered to Milan, Italy, to help Italian medical workers fight the coronavirus (COVID-19). The RescEU Reserve is coordinated and financed by the EU’s Civil Protection Mechanism and is hosted by Romania. This series shows the arrival of the masks in Milan and the departure from Italy of the Romanian medical team who has been sent to Lecco for two weeks, also coordinated by the EU Civil Protection Mechanism. - Multimedia Centre
