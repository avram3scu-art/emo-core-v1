5. Order restricting foreign trade in certain goods (medical protective equipment)

6. Safety goggles/visors, face shields, mouth-nose protection products, Class 2 ﬁltering face pieces, Class 3 ﬁltering face
pieces, protective gowns, protective suits, gloves

7. -

8. Ban on the export and shipment of goods that fall under the category of medical protective equipment (safety
goggles/visors, face shields, mouth and nose protection products, Class 2 ﬁltering face pieces, Class 3 ﬁltering face
pieces, protective gowns, protective suits, gloves).

9. As a result of the spread of the coronavirus, the demand for medical protective equipment is increasing. Production
increases are nowhere near able to meet this demand, and so we are at risk of having insuﬃcient coverage of these
goods, which are vitally important for health care. Measures introduced by other countries, such as speciﬁc trade
restrictions on individual goods in Thailand, South Korea, Taiwan, India and Kenya, among others, and nationwide

11. Yes

12. Following yesterday's announcement by French President Macron to requisition all existing stocks and production
capacities of respiratory masks for French health workers and French citizens infected with the coronavirus, appropriate
protective equipment can be expected to ﬂow out of the German market to France. Because the French measure has
immediately removed the supply of these goods from the French market, the French demand side (end users, other
private institutions and companies) will soon turn to readily available alternative procurement options. The German
market is a prime target for this type of evasion behaviour. To protect the functioning of the German health system and
the health of the German population, smooth, secure coverage of these fundamental medical protective equipment
goods is essential. Waiting any longer would considerably jeopardise the purpose of the measure since yesterday's
announcement of the requisitioning by the French President is likely to result in corresponding outﬂows of these goods
from the German to the French market.
